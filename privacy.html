ReadIt turns voice messages into text on your iPhone. It's built so that your messages stay yours.

Your voice messages

  • When you share a voice message to ReadIt, the audio file is copied into ReadIt's private storage on your iPhone.
  • ReadIt recognises the spoken language, writes out the message and, if needed, translates it into your chosen language. This uses Apple's speech recognition and Apple's Translation framework on your iPhone. ReadIt does not send your audio or text to us or to any other server.
  • The audio is deleted as soon as you close the result. If you haven't unlocked ReadIt Pro yet, a shared voice message stays on your iPhone until you read it in the ReadIt app. Leftover files from an interrupted session are deleted after 24 hours.
  • Transcripts and translations are only shown on screen. ReadIt doesn't keep a history of them.
  • The first time you use a language, iOS may download Apple language models for speech or translation. Those downloads come from Apple and contain nothing from your messages.

What ReadIt stores on your iPhone

  • Your chosen output language (Dutch, English or German).
  • A random identifier in the iPhone's keychain, noting that your one free session was used. It says nothing about you and stays on this iPhone.
  • Whether you have ReadIt Pro, based on Apple's verified purchase record.

Purchases

ReadIt Pro is a one-time In-App Purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. ReadIt only receives Apple's confirmation that the purchase is valid.

What ReadIt does not do

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No advertising, no tracking.
  • No analytics and no third-party SDKs.
  • No collection, sharing or sale of personal data.

Children

ReadIt doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Privacybeleid (samenvatting)

ReadIt zet spraakberichten om in tekst op je iPhone. Spraakherkenning en vertaling gebeuren met Apple-technologie op je iPhone; ReadIt stuurt geen audio of tekst naar ons of naar een andere server. Het audiobestand wordt gewist zodra je het resultaat sluit (of uiterlijk na 24 uur als iets werd onderbroken; een bericht dat wacht op ReadIt Pro blijft bewaard tot je het leest). ReadIt bewaart alleen je gekozen taal, een willekeurige code in de sleutelhanger dat je gratis keer is gebruikt, en of je ReadIt Pro hebt. Aankopen lopen volledig via Apple. Geen account, geen advertenties, geen tracking, geen analytics. Contact: bas@sebastianbronk.com.

ReadIt wandelt Sprachnachrichten auf deinem iPhone in Text um.

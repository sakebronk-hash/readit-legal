How to use ReadIt

  1. In WhatsApp (or any app that shares audio), long-press a voice message.
  2. Tap Forward, then the Share button.
  3. Choose ReadIt. You'll see the complete text, translated into your language if needed.

Languages

ReadIt understands Dutch, English and German, and shows text in the language you choose in the app under “Translate messages to”. If the spoken language was recognised wrongly, tap the caption under the text and choose the right one.

Free session and ReadIt Pro

Your first share is free. After that, ReadIt Pro unlocks unlimited voice messages for a one-time payment. There is no subscription. To get Pro back on a new iPhone, tap Restore purchases in the app. Got a code? Tap Redeem code.

ReadIt doesn't appear in the Share menu

Scroll to the end of the row of apps, tap More and switch on ReadIt. ReadIt only appears when you share audio.

Privacy

Speech recognition and translation happen on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
